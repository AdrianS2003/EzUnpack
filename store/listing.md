# Play Store listing copy

Character limits are Google's. Counts below were measured, not estimated —
the console rejects an overlong field rather than truncating it.

Write the Romanian version into the `ro-RO` locale and the English one into
`en-US`, and set `ro-RO` as the default if Romania is the first market.

## App name

**`EzUnpack`** — 8 of 30 characters.

Leave it as the bare name. "EzUnpack — ZIP & RAR Opener" would rank for more
searches, but the audience for this app is told its name by the person setting up
their phone; they are not searching for it.

## Full description

Written for the person who receives the archive, not for the person installing
the app for them — because the second one reads over the first one's shoulder.
No feature lists in jargon, and the limitation is stated rather than hidden.

**English** (1,318 of 4,000):

```
Somebody sent you a file and your phone will not open it. It ends in .zip or
.rar, and nothing happens when you tap it.

EzUnpack is for exactly that moment.

Open the archive, press one green button, and the photos and videos inside go
straight into your gallery — into their own EzUnpack album, so they are easy to
find and not lost among your own pictures. Documents stay in the app, where you
can read them, send them on, or copy them somewhere you choose.

Made for people who do not enjoy fighting with phones:

• One big green button per archive. No menus, no settings to get right first.
• Plain words. "Small file — 4 MB", not "4.2 MiB". It tells you where your files
  went, and shows you.
• Nothing gets lost. Every archive is kept in the app, so you can always find it
  again — and delete it when you are done.
• Romanian and English, following your phone.
• Light and dark, following your phone, and larger text for eyes that need it.
• Search by voice, if typing is hard.

Opens ZIP, RAR and 7z files.

No account. No advertising. Nothing is sent anywhere — the app has no internet
connection of its own, and everything stays on your phone.

One thing it cannot do: archives locked with a password. If the sender protected
it, EzUnpack cannot open it, and it will tell you so instead of failing quietly.
```

## Screenshots

Two is the minimum; four tells the story. Take them on a real phone, portrait,
with the Romanian interface if `ro-RO` is the default locale. Put a real archive
through the app first so nothing on screen is empty.

Shoot these, in this order — it is the actual journey:

1. **Home, with two or three archives in the feed.** The green UNPACK button has
   to be visible. This one screenshot is the whole pitch.
2. **The unpack sheet, showing what is inside.** File tiles plus the green "Save
   to Photos & Docs" button.
3. **The finished state** — the tick, "All files saved!", and the line saying
   where they went. This is the promise being kept.
4. **The folder screen**, with a document and its two labelled buttons.

Avoid: the empty home screen, the settings screen, and anything with a filename
like `Archive received 30.07.2026` in it.

Requirements: PNG or JPEG, 16:9 or 9:16, each side between 320 and 3,840 px.

## Feature graphic

1,024 × 500 px, no transparency, no rounded corners. It is shown above the
description and cropped on some layouts, so keep anything that matters in the
middle.

Simplest version that works: the wordmark centred on the brand blue `#386C96`,
or on white with the layered E to one side. Do not put the short description in
it as text — Google renders that separately and it ends up duplicated.
